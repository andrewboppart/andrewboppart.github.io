Privacy — Hermes Personal Integrations

Updated October 15, 2026. This integration is a private personal assistant for its owner's account.

Access and purpose. Depending on the owner's request, it can access Gmail messages and metadata, calendars and events, Drive files, Google Docs and Sheets contents, and contact information. Granted permissions also allow mail sending/modification and changes to calendars, files and documents; Contacts access is read-only. Permissions do not by themselves authorize an action: use is limited to the owner's explicit requests or already-authorized workflows.

Processing and storage. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's local Hermes profile. Relevant account information may also appear in local conversation history or logs. Relevant task content can be sent to configured AI providers for processing; the main assistant currently uses OpenAI, and auxiliary processing follows the configured providers. When the owner uses messaging or email services, such as Telegram, those services process the messages and responses sent through them. Data therefore does not necessarily remain on the owner's computer. Provider processing and retention depend on their applicable terms and settings.

Retention and control. No universal retention period or automatic deletion guarantee has been established for this setup. The owner can revoke the integration's access through Google Account connections and remove local credentials or history using the relevant controls. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself delete previously retained local content or provider records.

This website. Only these informational pages are published here, not private account content or credentials. The pages contain no analytics scripts or forms; the hosting service may process normal visitor connection information under its own policies.
