Hermes Personal Integrations

A private personal assistant operated by its owner, not a public service or an application offered for public signup.

The assistant connects the owner's Google account to help with requested or already-authorized tasks involving Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Contacts. Authorized capabilities include reading information and, where granted and specifically authorized, sending mail or making changes. Contacts access is read-only in the current integration.

Google account consent happens separately through Google's OAuth screens. This informational website has no Google-account login, account-data viewer or connection to the assistant's credentials.
